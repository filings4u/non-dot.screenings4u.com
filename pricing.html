Pricing FAQ

Current Workforce subscription pricing.

What are the current Employer prices?

Employer Essential is $85/month, Professional is $145/month, and Enterprise is $245/month.

What are the current C/TPA prices?

C/TPA Essential is $125/month, Professional is $225/month, and Enterprise is $375/month.

Are testing and screening services included?

No. The monthly subscription is for Workforce SaaS access. Fulfillment services are purchased separately where offered.

Which plan supports team users?

Employer Professional and Enterprise include team-user capability. C/TPA Professional and Enterprise also include team-user capability in the current catalog.

Which plan adds white-label features?

Employer Enterprise and C/TPA Enterprise include white-label capabilities. C/TPA Enterprise also includes SSO in the active entitlement set.
