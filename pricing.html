Pricing & Software FAQ

Current Workforce NON-DOT subscription pricing.

What are the current Employer software prices?

Employer Essential is $85/month, Professional is $145/month, and Enterprise is $245/month.

What are the current C/TPA software prices?

C/TPA Essential is $125/month, Professional is $225/month, and Enterprise is $375/month.

Are testing services included in the monthly software price?

No. Testing and other fulfillment services are purchased separately where offered.

Which plan includes enterprise controls?

Enterprise plans add the highest-level administrative, integration, branding, and audit capabilities enabled for that account type.
