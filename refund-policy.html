Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

This policy applies to screenings4u Workforce Compliance software subscriptions purchased through workforce.screenings4u.com.

Subscription cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of future recurring subscription billing. Cancellation does not automatically reverse charges already processed for an active billing period unless required by applicable law or agreed by screenings4u.

Testing and add-on services

Drug and alcohol testing and other add-on services are purchased separately from the software subscription. Refundability may depend on whether a service has been transmitted, scheduled, fulfilled, or incurred third-party costs.

Duplicate or billing errors

If you believe you were charged incorrectly or more than once, contact screenings4u promptly so the transaction can be reviewed.

How to request help

Contact support@screenings4u.com with your organization name, invoice or order reference, and the reason for the request.
